# Reduced-Order Surrogates for Forced Flexible Mesh Coastal-Ocean Models (2026)

- **Authors / venue:** F. H. Petersen, J. S. Mariegaard, R. Palmitessa, A. P. Engsig-Karup (DHI / DTU). arXiv preprint (elsarticle), Feb 2026, revised Apr 2026.
- **Links:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05416 · two of three datasets on Zenodo; **code not public** (IP reasons)
- **Source read:** tex (arXiv e-print)
- **Thread:** 1 ROM + sequence / 5 conditioning

## Summary
Reduced-order surrogates of a commercial shallow-water model (MIKE 21 FM) on **unstructured "flexible" meshes**, driven by **known time-varying external forcings** (wind, pressure, open-boundary conditions). General form: `x_{k+1} = Φ_D( M( Φ_E(x_k), Ψ_E(u_k), Ψ_E(u_{k+1}) ) )`, i.e. the forcing at **both k and k+1** is encoded and fed to the latent propagator. Compared: a **forced Koopman autoencoder** (linear K_f in latent space, trained end to end) vs **POD + {linear regression, MLP, GRU}**, with **eigenvalue regularisation** (penalise |λ|>1) and **temporal unrolling** (multi-step loss).

## Benchmark fields
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Input geometry | **Unstructured FV mesh, 3,320 / 8,533 / 28,158 elements** — the same scale as our 21,334 cells |
| Exogenous conditioning | **Central to the paper:** forcings at k and k+1 go through their own encoder Ψ_E, then into the propagator. This is exactly FlameBench's `phi(t)` + `phi(t+1)` contract |
| Latent reduction | POD (randomized SVD, as in our `PODReducer`) vs (Koopman) autoencoder; optional **variable-separated AEs** (smooth vs non-smooth fields encoded separately) |
| Prediction target | Next state via latent step (Koopman: linear) |
| Rollout & stability | Eigenvalue regularisation for linear propagators; **temporal unrolling** with backprop through N_TU steps; horizons up to **1 year at 30-min resolution** (~17.5k steps) |
| Demonstrated scale | 3 real coastal domains, multi-variable (S, U, V), multi-forcing |
| Datasets / physics | Øresund, Southern North Sea, Adriatic Sea (tides removed; meteorologically driven) |
| Code + license | Not public |
| Integration effort | **Low.** POD+LR/MLP/GRU are our `pod-arx`/`lstm` family; the Koopman AE is a small new class. Variable-separated encoding maps to our 11 fields (e.g. separate temperature/species from velocity) |

## Relevance to FlameBench
**The single closest methodological precedent in the list:** mesh-scale (21k-28k cells), unstructured, **forced by a known exogenous time series**, long AR horizons, with POD vs AE/Koopman compared head to head. Findings to test on flames: KAE beat POD surrogates in 2 of 3 cases; unrolling was needed for long-term stability. Concrete adoptions: (1) the `phi(t), phi(t+1)` encoding matches ours — cite it as prior art for the contract; (2) add eigenvalue regularisation + unrolling options to `pod-arx`/Koopman; (3) try **per-field-group encoders** for the 11 flame fields.
