## Summary
BLASTNet 2.0: 2.2 TB, 744 full-domain samples from 34 **3D DNS of reacting and non-reacting compressible turbulent flows** (skeletal finite-rate chemistry, mixture-averaged transport). The benchmark task is **3D super-resolution** (not forecasting): 49 variants of five approaches (RRDB, EDSR, RCAN, plus two scientific-ML variants with physics-based losses), with neural scaling analysis against tricubic interpolation. Findings: performance scales with model size and cost, architecture matters most at small sizes, and physics-based losses keep helping at scale.

## Relevance to FlameBench
**Positioning only.** It is the main existing *combustion* ML dataset, but for super-resolution of turbulent DNS, not forecasting a forced laminar flame on an unstructured mesh. Cite it to show the gap FlameBench fills. Useful details: SSIM + NRMSE per channel as local metrics; physics-consistent data augmentation (rotations preserving continuity); a documented normalisation pitfall (per-dataset mean/std failed on forced HIT) — relevant to our 11-field scaling in `scale_with_openmeasure`.
