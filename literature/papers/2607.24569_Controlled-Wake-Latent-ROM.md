# The balance between compactness and forecast accuracy of data-driven latent-space reduced-order models in controlled wake flows (2026)

- **Authors / venue:** A. Solera-Rico, P. García-Caspueñas, C. Sanmiguel Vila, S. Discetti (INTA / UC3M / Univ. Washington). arXiv preprint (JFM-style template), Jul 2026.

- **Source read:** tex (arXiv e-print)
- **Thread:** 1 ROM + sequence / 5 conditioning

## Summary
A systematic study of **how the choice of spatial encoder affects the predictability of the latent dynamics under control inputs**. Two actuated 2D wakes (simplified truck at Re=500 with **filtered-random broadband actuation**; fluidic pinball at Re=150 with multi-input step ramps). Encoders: POD, convolutional AE, β-VAE, decomposed-KL VAE. Predictors: three LSTM variants fed with [latent, control] over a lookback window — (i) single-step AR, (ii) direct sequence (LSTM context + future control sequence → MLP → whole horizon), (iii) derivative-based (the LSTM predicts dz/dt, integrated with 2nd-order Adams–Bashforth). SINDy is in the appendix. **Key finding: AEs compress better and reconstruct sharper, but give irregular, broadband latent dynamics that diverge sooner. POD gives smoother latent trajectories and more reliable long-horizon forecasts.**

## Benchmark fields
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Input geometry | 2D DNS fields (CNN encoders on a grid; POD on any mesh) |
| Exogenous conditioning | **Control signal b_t concatenated with the latent state at every lookback step; future controls are fed explicitly** (in the direct-sequence variant, for the whole horizon). Includes a broadband random excitation, like a sine sweep in spirit |
| Latent reduction | POD vs CAE vs β-VAE vs dKL-VAE (the object of study) |
| Prediction target | Next latent (AR), whole horizon (direct), or latent derivative (AB2 integration) |
| Rollout & stability | Long-horizon divergence probability; effective divergence rate (Rosenstein-style); phase/amplitude analysis |
| Demonstrated scale | 2D low-Re DNS |
| Datasets / physics | Actuated truck wake, fluidic pinball |
| Code + license | Not yet released |
| Integration effort | **Low**: our `lstm` / `pod-transformer` / `ae-transformer` already cover the encoder x predictor grid. Adding the "direct sequence" and "derivative + AB2" heads is a small change |

## Relevance to FlameBench
**Directly shapes the benchmark design.** (1) It predicts that our `ae-transformer` may beat `pod-transformer` on one-step error but lose on long rollouts — FlameBench should report **both short and long-horizon metrics** to expose this trade-off. (2) Its encoder x predictor factorial design is a clean template for the FlameBench tables (POD/AE/β-VAE x LSTM/Transformer/ARX). (3) Its divergence-rate and phase/amplitude diagnostics suit a periodically forced flame.
