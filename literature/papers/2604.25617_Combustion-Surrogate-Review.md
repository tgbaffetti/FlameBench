## Summary
A broad review of ML surrogates in combustion across scales: ML interatomic potentials and reaction-network discovery; AI-enhanced diagnostics (reconstruction/tomography); CFD-scale closures, chemistry acceleration (neural surrogates, operator learning, conservation constraints), flow-field surrogates and digital twins; PINN/hybrid methods. Includes a quantitative synthesis of reported accuracy–speed trade-offs and a multi-metric "scorecard", and ends with an agentic-AI outlook.

## Relevance to FlameBench
**Positioning and motivation, not models.** §5.2 explicitly calls for **open benchmark suites** and reporting standards in combustion ML: clear baselines and hardware; separate training, inference and end-to-end timings; errors on **decision-relevant quantities** rather than only pointwise errors; conservation diagnostics and **long-time integration stability**; memory/latency. This is a ready-made citation for why FlameBench exists and which columns its results tables should have. Its flow-field-surrogate section (§3.3.3) is thin on *time-resolved field forecasting*, which confirms the gap.
