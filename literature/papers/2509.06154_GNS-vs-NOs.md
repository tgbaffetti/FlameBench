# Data-Efficient Time-Dependent PDE Surrogates: Graph Neural Simulators vs. Neural Operators (2025)

- **Authors / venue:** D. Nayak, S. Goswami (Johns Hopkins). arXiv preprint, Sep 2025.
- **Links:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06154 · code https://github.com/Centrum-IntelliPhysics/GNS_vs_NOs
- **Source read:** tex (arXiv e-print)
- **Thread:** 3 GNNs (+ 6 evaluation)

## Summary
A controlled comparison in the **low-data regime**. A GNS (message passing that predicts the instantaneous time derivative, integrated by an explicit scheme) is compared with FNO and DeepONet variants (full-rollout, autoregressive, time-integrated) on 2D Burgers (scalar and coupled), Allen–Cahn and nonlinear shallow water. GNS gets <1% relative L2 with only **30 training trajectories** (3% of the data) and much less AR error growth (82.5% lower than FNO, 99.9% lower than DeepONet). Training trajectories are chosen by PCA(20)+KMeans.

## Relevance to FlameBench
**Evidence for the data regime.** FlameBench has very few trajectories (2 sine sweeps per config). This paper argues that local, derivative-predicting GNNs generalise best with little data, while REALM finds GNNs weak on real combustion meshes. **FlameBench can arbitrate this conflict** — a concrete research question for the paper. Also worth borrowing: report error-vs-rollout-time curves for every model, and "DeepONet-AR vs time-integrated DeepONet" as variants.
