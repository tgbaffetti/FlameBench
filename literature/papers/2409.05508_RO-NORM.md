# A general reduced-order neural operator for spatio-temporal predictive learning on complex spatial domains — RO-NORM (2024)

- **Authors / venue:** Q. Meng, Y. Li, Z. Deng, X. Liu, G. Chen, Q. Wu, C. Liu, et al. arXiv preprint (Elsevier template), Sep 2024.
- **Links:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05508 · code+data https://github.com/qingluM/RO-NORM
- **Source read:** tex (arXiv e-print)
- **Thread:** 2 operators / 5 conditioning

## Summary
Targets **"unequal-domain" mappings**, e.g. a **time signal → spatio-temporal field** (`P(t) → V(x,t)`) or a spatial field → spatio-temporal field. Pre-computed (POD-like) temporal/spatial bases rewrite the spatio-temporal function as a sum of products of bases and weight functions. This turns the task into a same-domain mapping, solved by **NORM** (a neural operator on Riemannian manifolds using Laplace–Beltrami eigenfunctions as the spectral basis on the mesh). Compared with DeepONet, POD-DeepONet, PCA-Net and vanilla NORM.

## Benchmark fields
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Input geometry | **Native unstructured mesh / manifold** (Laplace–Beltrami eigenbasis of the mesh replaces the FFT) |
| Exogenous conditioning | **The input *is* the time signal**: the blood-flow case maps inlet/outlet pressure & velocity histories P(t) to the 3D velocity field V(x,t) in an aorta — structurally identical to phi(t) → flame field |
| Latent reduction | POD temporal/spatial bases + LBO spectral basis |
| Prediction target | Whole spatio-temporal field (non-autoregressive) |
| Rollout & stability | None — no AR error accumulation, but fixed-length time windows |
| Demonstrated scale | 2D Burgers, wave, heat-source layout, composite workpiece temperature/deformation, **3D aorta blood flow** (FE meshes) |

## Relevance to FlameBench
Introduces a **second task formulation** worth offering in the benchmark: *forcing sequence → response sequence* (non-AR, fixed window), next to next-step AR. For a sine-sweep-forced flame this is the natural "system identification" view, and it sidesteps rollout instability. The LBO-eigenbasis trick is also the **cleanest way to get an FNO-like spectral operator on our unstructured mesh** without interpolation (an alternative to GINO). Optional / second-tier candidate.
