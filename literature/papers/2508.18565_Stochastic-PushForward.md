## Summary
**Stochastic PushForward (SPF)** for **latent ROMs** (convolutional AE + latent sequence model). Between epochs, the current model builds a *supplementary dataset* by rolling out δ steps from each training state. Training stays **one-step-ahead**, with inputs drawn stochastically from the ground truth and from these self-predicted states, plus a loss coefficient balancing the two. It gets multi-step robustness **without holding unrolled graphs in GPU memory**, and balances short- vs long-term accuracy. Tested on 2D Burgers and shallow water, with limited-data, timestep-extrapolation and noisy-data studies.

## Relevance to FlameBench
The most **drop-in stabilisation method for our latent-space models**, with constant memory. Suggested as a `training.stabilization = "spf"` option alongside "none", "noise" and "pushforward" in `train.json`, so every sequence model in the benchmark can be compared with and without it.
