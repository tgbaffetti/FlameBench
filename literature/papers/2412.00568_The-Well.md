# The Well: a Large-Scale Collection of Diverse Physics Simulations for Machine Learning (2024)

- **Authors / venue:** R. Ohana, M. McCabe, et al. (Polymathic AI / Flatiron Institute and many domain collaborators). NeurIPS 2024 Datasets & Benchmarks.
- **Links:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00568 · code+data https://github.com/PolymathicAI/the_well (data **CC-BY-4.0**)
- **Source read:** tex (arXiv e-print)
- **Thread:** 4 benchmarks / 6 evaluation

## Summary
15 TB, 16 datasets of spatiotemporal simulations (acoustics, active matter, Euler multi-quadrant, Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion, MHD, planetary shallow water, neutron-star merger, Rayleigh–Bénard, Rayleigh–Taylor, shear flow, supernova, turbulent radiative layers, viscoelastic flow, …) with a unified PyTorch loader and evaluation library. Baselines: FNO, TFNO, U-Net, **CNextU-Net** (~15–20M params, AdamW, 12 h on one H100).

## Benchmark fields
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Input geometry | Regular grids (some in non-Cartesian coordinates) |
| Exogenous conditioning | Constant simulation parameters per trajectory; no time-varying forcing |

| Prediction target | Next step; rollout windows (6–12, 13–30) |

## Relevance to FlameBench
Borrow: (1) **VRMSE** (RMSE normalised by the field variance, so predicting the mean scores 1) — a scale-free, per-field metric well suited to our 11 heterogeneous fields; (2) **per-field breakdown** and **spectral-band error growth** plots; (3) **one-step vs windowed rollout** reporting, since model rankings flip between the two; (4) time-boxed, capacity-matched training as a fairness rule. Positioning: The Well has no combustion and no exogenously forced systems, which FlameBench fills.
